Hi, I'm adlerzei. I build software, and I like it best when other people can actually understand it.
{{< /lead >}}

By day I work at [Advancis Software & Services](https://www.advancis.net/) near Frankfurt. I lead a small
team that connects WinGuard, a platform for security and building management, to hardware from all
kinds of manufacturers. I'm also the person in charge of our technical documentation. That means
strategy, standards, a guild of about ten colleagues, and a docs pipeline built on Hugo, just like
this site.

Before that I spent quite a few years at TU Darmstadt. I finished with two Master's degrees, one in
IT security and one in computer science (with distinction), and on the side I mentored students and
ran workshops for them.
